# Game attributions

This site hosts a mixed collection of preserved Flash games, HTML5 games, Unity
WebGL builds, and links to externally hosted games. The following credits are
based on the information available in the downloaded project and should be
reviewed before public distribution.

- Bomber At War series — ConArtist Games
- Warfare 1917/1944 — ConArtist Games
- Swords and Sandals / Sands of Coliseum — 3RD Sense
- Learn to Fly series — Light Bringer Games
- Hex Empire — Oddgoo
- Bloons Tower Defense 5 — Ninja Kiwi
- Age of War 2 — Max Games
- Papa's Pizzeria — Flipline Studios
- Awesome Tanks 2 — Alexander Gette
- Stick War series — Jason Whitham and Brock White
- Endless War series — Vitaly Zaborov

These games are presented through [Ruffle](https://ruffle.rs/). Ruffle is a
separate open-source Flash emulator and is not the owner of the games.

## HTML5 and Unity games

The local web builds include community remakes, fan projects, ports, and
original web games. Known credits include Orteil (Cookie Clicker), Griffpatch
(Appel/Scratch), Blue Wizard Digital (Basket Bros and Shell Shockers), Juicy
Beast (Burrito Bison), Nils Asejevs (Cubefield), Oslo Albet (Fireboy and
Watergirl), Bennett Foddy (Getting Over It), Splapp-Me-Do (The Impossible
Quiz), and Oskar Stålberg (Townscaper).

The Unity entries include Slope, Subway Surfers, and Deathrun 3D. Their
original creators and licensors retain ownership of their names, brands, and
content.

## External games

Some catalog entries link to third-party hosts instead of shipping a local
copy. The current catalog identifies Basket Bros (`freegamesonlinee.github.io`),
Crazy Cattle 3D (`nealfun.app`), and a Scratch version of Five Nights at
Freddy's as external content. These destinations can change independently of
this repository.

## Hosting and removal requests

No ownership or licensing rights are claimed by this repository. Before
publishing or monetizing the site, verify the license or permission for every
game and asset. If a rights holder requests removal or correction, remove the
affected catalog entry/assets and record the change in the maintenance notes.

The [maintenance guide](maintenance.md) contains the release checklist and
the [GitHub migration guide](github-migration.md) contains repository setup
instructions.
